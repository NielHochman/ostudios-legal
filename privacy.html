Privacy Policy — Bonk

O Studios · Effective 10 September 2026

Bonk is a real-time player-versus-player mobile game made by O Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who we share it with, and how you can delete it. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed: it is short on purpose.

1. What we collect

DataWhyWhere it lives
An anonymous account ID created the first time you open the game So your progress, trophies and league standing follow you between sessions Supabase (EU, Ireland)
A display name generated automatically (e.g. Baruto#2221) Shown to opponents and on the leaderboard. It is not your real name and you never have to enter one Supabase
Gameplay data — match results, scores, hits, trophies, win streaks, league tier, unlocked stickers, in-app button taps To run matches, rank leagues, award stickers, and understand how the game is played so we can improve it Supabase
Facebook login data — your Facebook user ID, name and email address, only if you choose "Play with Facebook" To link your anonymous account to you so you can recover it on a new phone. We do not post to Facebook or read your friends list Supabase
Technical data — device model, operating system version, app version, screen size, frame rate, crash and error reports To find bugs and performance problems on specific devices GameAnalytics
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) Used by our advertising partner to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete it in your Android settings Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks
Purchase records — what you bought and a purchase token, never your card details To deliver what you paid for and to restore purchases Google Play; a receipt in Supabase
IP address, transiently, as part of any internet request Required to deliver the service; used for security and to approximate region Our hosting providers' logs, briefly

We do not collect your contacts, your precise location, your photos, or the contents of your messages. Friend invites work with a short code you share yourself; we never read your contact list.

2. Notifications

With your permission, Bonk can show a local reminder on your device. These are scheduled on your phone and are not sent from our servers. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in your Android notification settings at any time.

3. Who we share data with

We use these providers to run the game. Each receives only what it needs for its job, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — database and account hosting (EU region). Policy
  • GameAnalytics — anonymous usage and performance statistics. Policy
  • Unity LevelPlay (by Unity / ironSource) and the ad networks it mediates — in-game advertising. Policy
  • Google Play — app distribution and in-app purchases. Policy
  • Meta Platforms — only if you sign in with Facebook. Policy

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything attached to it from inside the game: Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account, match history, trophies, league standing, stickers and any linked Facebook identity are all removed from our servers, and the game verifies that nothing remains before confirming.

You can also email us (address below) and we will do it for you within 30 days. Anonymous statistics that cannot be linked back to you may be kept. See Delete your account for step-by-step instructions.

5. How long we keep data

For as long as your account exists. Accounts that have not been opened for 24 months may be deleted. Purchase records are kept as long as the law requires.

6. Children

Bonk is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and CCPA) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Most of this you can do yourself in the game; for anything else, email us.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to our database is restricted and audited. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you as required by law if a breach affects your data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the game.
