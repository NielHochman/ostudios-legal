Delete your Bonk account

O Studios

In the game (immediate)

  1. Open Bonk.
  2. Tap the menu button (☰) in the top-right corner, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete.

Your account, match history, trophies, league standing, stickers and any linked Facebook identity are deleted from our servers immediately and permanently. The game checks that nothing remains before it confirms. This cannot be undone.

By email

If you cannot open the game — for example you no longer have the phone — email niel@ostudios.games or maayan@ostudios.games with the subject "Delete my Bonk account". If you signed in with Facebook, send it from the email address on your Facebook account so we can match it. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account ID, display name, Facebook link, all match records, trophies, league entries, stickers, and in-app event logs tied to your account.
  • Kept: aggregate statistics that cannot be linked back to you (for example "how many matches were played on Tuesday"), and purchase records for as long as tax and consumer law require.

See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.
